Scope

Audience insight / Streaming adoption

A humorous campaign built around an emerging streaming habit: watching several episodes at a time.

The challenge

Rebuild consumer trust and help customers understand Netflix’s transition from DVDs by mail to streaming.

The approach

Start with a simple audience insight: early streaming adopters were watching three to five episodes in a sitting. Build a humorous campaign around that behavior.

My contribution

My task was to support the transition to streaming. Our team developed a campaign that brought binge-watching into Netflix’s brand story.
