Thursday Night Football

Making Prime Video part of game night.

Client / Platform

Prime Video

Focus

Creative leadership

Scope

Platform positioning / Live NFL

Creative leadership across multiple seasons of NFL football, helping establish a new streaming destination for sports fans.

The challenge

Amazon entered Thursday Night Football streaming in 2017. The challenge was to establish Prime Video as a credible destination for NFL fans while building awareness of its broader streaming offering.

The approach

Build campaigns that made Amazon’s arrival in football unmistakable and gave viewers a clear connection between the NFL and Prime Video.

My contribution

I served as creative lead across multiple seasons, developing campaigns to introduce Amazon’s NFL offering and build awareness of Prime Video.
